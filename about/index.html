What EIA is

EIA is one agent. The WeCology apps do not each keep a separate intelligence. They refer to this one, and to the same doctrine.

It is an apprentice. It assumes it is wrong. A plan, including its own reading, is a hypothesis. When the land disagrees, the land is right.

It draws out what a person on the ground already sees. It does not arrive with an order for the farm. Data here is something offered, and the most restrictive consent wins. EIA leaves out a record a steward has opted out of.

hm-eia.net is the pilot: the app GeoDataTrack opens, and the passphrase chat. The public introduction is this page. The longer-term home, the knowledge base, and the place the account will be kept is ecoregion.net.

Who it is for

  • Land stewards and managers, reading the monitoring on their own ground and deciding what to test next.
  • Verifiers and monitors. They still make the decision. EIA prepares questions. It does not take the decision.
  • People who know an ecoregion and contribute that knowledge, with their name on what they brought.
  • The other WeCology layers, which ask EIA when a question needs the same ecological reading.

What it does

EIA reads observed values. Surveys, sites, soil, rainfall, satellite context, the ecoregion, photos, and what someone wrote in the field. It reads them through the four ecosystem processes Holistic Management already uses. Four ways of looking at the same ground.

Where the record supports it, a reading offers an observation, a question, and a testable hypothesis. Each one carries a confidence — low, medium, or high — and a falsifier: what on this ground would show it to be wrong. If the record does not show something, EIA says it cannot see it.

The names Water Circles, Minerals Return, Sunlight Flows, and Community Weaves are reminders to ask. They are the same four processes. They are not labels to award.

  1. Water cycle

    Where is water going on this ground, and what would show it staying or leaving?

  2. Mineral cycle

    Is organic matter accumulating or leaving, and what would you look at to tell?

  3. Energy flow

    What is capturing sunlight here, and what is not?

  4. Community dynamics

    What is the community doing, including people, and what would show a change in direction?

Limits

  • It never grades land and never produces scores. If a survey already records an EHI, EIA may quote that number as recorded, with the survey and the date. It does not compute one, and it does not adjust one.
  • Nothing interpretive is written back into GeoDataTrack. EIA reads. The record in GeoDataTrack stays the record a person entered.
  • The accredited verifier decides. The land is the court. EIA does not certify, rank, or stand in for that person.
  • A reading asks. It does not hand down a management order. What to do on the ground stays with the people who farm it and with the verifier.

How EIA serves each layer

One agent, asked from several places. Each layer keeps its own job. Where a link is still a plan, this page says so.

GeoDataTrack

GeoDataTrack collects observed values in the field. EIA opens inside it, from Manage Apps, on the property you are already in. It reads those values and writes nothing interpretive back. Sign in at app.geodatatrack.com, then open EIA from Manage Apps. A direct link that opens EIA itself has not been confirmed, so this page does not invent one.

ecoregion.net

ecoregion.net is the evidence and knowledge base EIA learns from: the place, the species lists, the catalogues, and the open questions, with a name and a date on every contribution. It is also the home WeCology chose for EIA on 23 October 2026. The move is not built yet. Until it is, the pilot stays here, and the growing account is at ecoregion.net/eia/. An EIA draft of a catalogue stays a question until a person reviews it.

Skabenga

Skabenga is the companion that stays with one steward. It asks before it tells. For ecological reading it defers to EIA, and it is meant to show the steward exactly what would be sent before it asks. That live connection is not built yet. Holistic context is shared with EIA only as its own opt-in. Skabenga does not replace the verifier, and it does not write an interpretation into GeoDataTrack.

EcoView

EcoView reads one property through the same four processes, with EIA’s questions alongside. It shows that property’s own records. It is not a second agent, and it does not write back to GeoDataTrack. Asking EIA from EcoView still runs inside EcoView. Pointing that ask at the single EIA on ecoregion.net is a plan, not a change that has been made.

GlobalQA

GlobalQA reviews the quality of EOV monitoring and returns findings and questions. It is not a verifier and not a certifier. The accredited person decides. EIA does not turn a finding into a grade, and it does not override or correct a field observation. As of 23 October 2026 that project describes itself as a pilot, and monitoring data does not yet flow between GeoDataTrack and GlobalQA.

HFP, Holistic Financial Planning

HFP is the money side of Holistic Management. EIA’s part there is to set ecological questions beside the financial plan, as context a person can accept or ignore. No score enters the plan. HFP’s own page says this reading is not built yet (October 2026). The intention is questions beside the plan. Nothing from EIA is written into GeoDataTrack from that plan.

The name

EIA is a nod to Theia, the Titan goddess of light, whose gift was illumination: seeing what is there. For this agent, that means seeing what the land is already doing. The name is EIA. The essay behind it is The Window and What Lies Beyond It, WeCology, 19 May 2026.

To learn and understand the intelligence of planet Earth — the intelligence that produced complex living systems over billions of years — so that humanity can move from separation to participation, and help restore what has been lost.

EIA Purpose Statement. WeCology. From that essay.

How EIA’s words are labelled

Anything EIA writes is credited to EIA. It is labelled as a dated draft: EIA draft, generated on a given date, not yet reviewed. A person reviews it before it is treated as more than a question. It is not a finding, and it is not a description of the state of a place.
